Why the Gawad Lab

What you get here that is hard to find elsewhere

Invent, then ship

The methods developed here (PTA, paired genome-and-transcriptome, single-microbe genomics) are used by labs worldwide and have been commercialized through BioSkryb Genomics. Your work has a path from bench to product to patient.

Questions from the clinic

The PI is a practicing pediatric oncologist. Projects start with a real patient problem and have access to clinical samples through Stanford Children's Health and collaborators at St. Jude.

Wet lab and dry lab under one roof

Chemistry, molecular biology, sequencing, and computational analysis sit together, so you learn the whole pipeline and own your project end to end.

Stanford and the Bay Area

Bio-X, the Chan Zuckerberg Biohub, Stanford Genomics cores, and a dense biotech community within a few miles of the lab.

Mentorship toward your next step

Alumni have gone on to faculty, staff-scientist, and industry leadership roles, medical school, and MD/PhD programs. We plan for your career from day one.

Room to take risks

Support from the NIH Director's New Innovator program and foundation awards funds high-risk method development that would not fit a conventional grant.
