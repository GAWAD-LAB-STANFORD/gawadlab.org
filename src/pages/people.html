The people behind the methods

The lab is starting a new chapter and building its next team. We are looking for people who want to do serious science at the boundary of technology and pediatric oncology, and who want to do it in a place where colleagues support each other.

We are growing

Come build the next generation of single-cell tools with us

With new support in place, we are recruiting scientists at every career stage over the coming year. Early members of a growing lab get unusual ownership: you shape the projects, the methods, and the culture. The culture we want is simple: rigorous, generous, and fun to be part of.
